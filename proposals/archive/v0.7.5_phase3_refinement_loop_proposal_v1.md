# v0.7.5 Ph3 Refinement-Loop Proposal — Eight-Part Package

## 0. Framing

This document files **eight proposed changes** to the `research-writing-harness-claude` plugin targeted at **v0.7.5 consideration**. The package is the direct successor of the v0.7.4 economic-efficiency package (proposals P-1 through P-8, filed 2026-04-21 and shipped in v0.7.4), and its proposals are numbered P-9 through P-16 to preserve a continuous proposal namespace across the two releases.

The v0.7.4 package addressed a dispatch-and-artefact-economy problem: the same round was too expensive because its dispatch was unprincipled and its artefacts were prose-first. The v0.7.5 package addresses a distinct problem one layer up: **the Ph3 rung itself conflates two workloads whose amortization profiles are incompatible**. Refinement iteration (the many-shot inner loop) must amortize at the speed of local change; submission-readiness certification (the single-shot terminal audit) must amortize over the full manuscript. Packing both into Ph3 meant every local edit paid a global-audit cost it did not need, and Ph4 paid for checks that had already been spent.

The redesign is an **AORE-style role disaggregation** between Ph3 and Ph4. Ph3 keeps only the softgoals iteration can move — convergence and integrity. Ph4 absorbs the terminal-certification softgoals that neither improve nor decay with iteration. The checks still run; they run at the rung where they are dispositive. No invariant is demoted. The four-agent contract, the Grounding Protocol, the 15-field `SectionStateObject`, the 30-trigger enum, the MCR admission gate, the Check 8 TerminalSignoffRow binding, the `E-MA-CAPABILITY-INVERSION` refusal, and the I-SubAgent-1 invariant are all untouched. Every change is internal to the Ph3 envelope shape and the Ph3 ↔ Ph4 division of labour.

The package is shippable in two idempotent passes consistent with v0.7.4 discipline. Pass 1 is scaffold (schema extensions and dispatch-plan fields); Pass 2 is substantive (default-profile rewire, parallel subagent contract, carryover cache, Reflector recurrence-widening). Dual-read through the v0.7.5 minor; removed at v0.7.6 RC.

## 1. Evidence base: the Ph3-as-submission-audit cost structure

The v0.7.4 package's evidence base was a single-round observation — the INF3006Y iter-7 stability pass — and that n=1 caveat carries visibly into the v0.7.4 CHANGELOG. The v0.7.5 package's evidence base is structural rather than empirical, and the distinction is deliberate: the problem this package addresses is **architectural** — it follows from the shape of the current Ph3 envelope, not from a cost measurement — so the falsification test lives at §7 (Instrumentation) rather than at §1.

The structural claim decomposes into four observations about the current (v0.7.4.1) Ph3 envelope:

**(a) Check-surface invariance under iteration.** The current Ph3 envelope runs the full SAFEGUARD layer (checks 1/4/5/7/8), the Coupling E.2 graph-grounding overlay on the full citation set, the seven-step Evaluator judgment pass on the full file, and (when invoked) the IS-theory, Suchman, narrative-structure, and abstract-body passes on every iteration. Most of these checks return the same verdict on every iteration the manuscript is not mutated in the check's locality. The IS-theory pass does not change its five-element-model verdict when a single paragraph is tightened; the Suchman register audit does not change its seven-move inventory when a citation is re-paginated; the graph-grounding overlay does not reshape when the bibliography is stable. These checks are *submission-readiness* checks: their purpose is to certify that the manuscript will survive external review, and they are invariant across the local edits that constitute a refinement iteration.

**(b) Check-surface variance under iteration.** A smaller subset of checks does change verdict round-over-round. The grounding audit surfaces different findings as paragraphs are rewritten. The Check 8 accessibility overlay's Cadence-Flag, First-Use-Flag, and Signpost-Flag fire on local prose surface in ways that track local edits. The sentence-level pass (Bacon 9-point) and the contradiction check on newly introduced citation pairs move with local change. These are the checks whose *value* is highest inside the iteration loop because iteration is what moves their verdict.

**(c) Ph4-entry finding surprise.** Because the submission-readiness checks run at Ph3 today, they run under a dispatch that is not yet in the submission-readiness register. The Evaluator's Ph3 prompt frames each check as part of the convergence loop; its Ph4 prompt frames each check as terminal certification. The two framings produce different adversarial postures, and the Ph3-framed execution can surface incidentally-detected issues that are not dispositive at Ph3 but would have been dispositive at Ph4. The current envelope catches these issues *and* requires the Evaluator to file them as Ph3 findings, inflating the Ph3 findings register with signal that was never going to close at Ph3.

**(d) Iteration-rate ceiling.** The combined effect is an iteration rate ceiling. A many-iteration refinement loop wants wall-clock per iteration to be bounded by the cost of checks that move with the diff. The current envelope makes wall-clock per iteration bounded by the cost of the full submission audit. The user's directive — *"the speed and accuracy of each iteration matter"* — is incompatible with this ceiling, and the ceiling is not a cost measurement; it is a consequence of the check-surface packaging.

The v0.7.4 stability sub-mode (P-2) is the one existing counterweight. It collapses the envelope to grounding-plus-Check-8 when the manuscript is byte-stable. But P-2 is admitted only on byte-stability, which is the degenerate case. Between active refinement and byte-stability there is a large operating regime — small-delta rounds, where five percent of the file moved — for which no reduced envelope exists today. The v0.7.5 package fills this regime with a profile-conditioned default that defaults to refine, escalates to structural or deep only when the dispatch plan flags it, and inherits from P-2's hash-stability discipline rather than replacing it.

## 2. Theoretical reframe — the Ph3/Ph4 softgoal decomposition

Under a Strategic Rationale view, the softgoals Ph3 is currently asked to satisfice are three, not one:

- **Convergence** — the manuscript's prose surface stabilizes against a declared metric.
- **Integrity** — grounding, accessibility, and the non-negotiable register commitments hold at every iteration.
- **Submission-readiness** — the manuscript will survive external peer review on a breadth-first basis: IS-theory alignment, Suchman register discipline (where applicable), narrative arc, abstract-body coverage, bibliography contradiction integrity, graph-grounded citation alignment.

The first two are *iteration-shaped*: iteration moves the dial, and absence of iteration lets them decay. The third is *terminal-shaped*: it neither improves with iteration nor decays without it, and iteration is the wrong instrument for moving it. Packing all three into Ph3 is therefore not a cost problem but a role-assignment problem: the Ph3 adversarial agent has been asked to run two incompatible protocols simultaneously.

The redesign assigns the three softgoals to their rungs cleanly. Ph3 carries convergence and integrity; Ph4 absorbs submission-readiness. The Ph3 Evaluator operates in a refinement register — depth-first on local change, fast, high-iteration. The Ph4 Evaluator operates in a certification register — breadth-first on the full manuscript, slow, single-shot (or few-shot under the +50% MCR reserve). The separation is load-bearing in the same way the Generator-Evaluator separation is load-bearing: the same agent cannot carry two protocols without collapsing one into the other.

Two corollaries follow. First, the Ph3 iteration budget becomes economically rational: every iteration pays only for checks whose value is highest during iteration. Second, the Ph4 admission is no longer a formality gated on MCR — it is an active phase change where the adversarial register shifts from *is this stable?* to *is this defensible?* The Ph4 envelope remains the strict superset of Ph3 that v0.7.4.1 specifies; what changes is that the superset-minus-Ph3 surface now carries dispositive content rather than ratification of what Ph3 already spent budget on.

## 3. Proposals

### P-9. Ph3 / Ph4 check-surface disaggregation

**Claim.** The following checks should default to **Ph4** rather than Ph3: (a) full SAFEGUARD checks 1, 4, 5, 7 run depth-first; (b) IS-theory pass (Baird 9-step); (c) Suchman register audit; (d) public-interest accountability pass (when the project opts in); (e) Coupling E.2 graph-grounding overlay as a breadth sweep over the full citation set; (f) narrative-structure pass (Sexton 10-item arc); (g) check-abstract-body audit; (h) full-bibliography contradiction audit. Each remains **available at Ph3 as an explicit opt-in** through the F6 dispatch plan's `check_profile: Ph3-deep` declaration (see P-11). The checks that stay at Ph3 by default are the ones whose verdict is iteration-shaped: the grounding audit (non-negotiable full-file floor), the Check 8 accessibility overlay (binding at TerminalSignoffRow — the §13 commitment is load-bearing inside the iteration loop because accessibility drift is iteration-shaped), the sentence-level pass (Bacon 9-point) on changed paragraphs, the contradiction check on newly introduced citation pairs, and a P-stage register spot-check.

**Change surface.**

- `skills/run-phase-3/SKILL.md` Phase 3 (Evaluator Engagement) is rewritten: the canonical Ph3 envelope is the **refine** profile; the **structural** and **deep** profiles are invoked only on dispatch-plan declaration.
- `skills/run-phase-4/SKILL.md` Phase 3 absorbs the demoted checks as **mandatory** (not optional) Ph4 passes. The Ph4 envelope already contains most of these as part of its "strict superset" framing; the change is to mark them as Ph4-primary rather than Ph3-carried-forward.
- `references/PHASE_PROTOCOL.md §3.3` (Ph3 contract) is rewritten to enumerate the three profiles and to name the demoted checks explicitly as "Ph4-default, Ph3-opt-in".
- `references/REVIEW_ORCHESTRATION.md` Steps 0.2 (Coupling E.2 overlay), 4 (SAFEGUARD layer), and 5 (theoretical-framework overlays) gain phase-conditioned gating: at Ph3 these steps run only when the dispatch profile is `structural` or `deep`; at Ph4 they run unconditionally.
- `agents/evaluator.md` gains a `§Adversarial register` section that names the two protocols (refinement / certification) and binds the agent's posture to the active phase.

**Invariant preserved.** Full-file grounding floor at every iteration. Check 8 at TerminalSignoffRow. Generator-as-sole-writer. User-gated checkpoints. The four-agent contract. The demoted checks still run — they run at Ph4, and Ph4 admission under MCR already requires every section to be `Ph3_converged`, so no section can reach submission without having paid for the demoted checks exactly once.

**Invariant strengthened.** The adversarial register at each rung becomes explicit rather than implicit. The Ph3 Evaluator's framing aligns with the softgoal the rung is actually moving.

**Evidence.** Structural — §1(a,b,c,d) above. The v0.7.4.1 `skills/run-phase-3-stability/SKILL.md` already implements the principle for the degenerate byte-stable case; P-9 generalizes it to the full refinement regime.

**Priority.** Highest-leverage. P-9 is the architectural core of the package; P-10 through P-16 instrument and tighten around it.

### P-10. Diff-scoped Evaluator with a full-file grounding floor

**Claim.** The grounding layer and the judgment layer have fundamentally different locality structures. Grounding must read the full file — GROUNDING_PROTOCOL Rule 1 is absolute, and this floor is load-bearing. Judgment over stable prose is waste — re-judging an unchanged paragraph pays cost for signal already established. The redesign separates the two scopes explicitly: grounding runs on the full file on every Ph3 iteration; judgment runs only on paragraphs whose SHA-256 has changed since the prior Ph3 round.

**Change surface.**

- `references/PHASE_PROTOCOL.md §3.3.4` (manuscript-hash contract, currently supporting P-2 at whole-manuscript granularity) extends to **paragraph-level hash map**: for each section, a `paragraph_hash_map: { paragraph_id: SHA-256 }` field in `convergence_journal.jsonl` row.
- `scripts/paragraph_hash_map.py` (new) computes the map from `manuscript/main.md` at Planner Phase 0.6 (immediately before the F6 dispatch plan is emitted) and writes it to the prospective row of the journal.
- `agents/evaluator.md` Phase 3.2 (Judgment-layer execution) gains the **diff-scope rule**: the Evaluator receives (i) the full file as grounding substrate, (ii) the changed-paragraph set computed as hash-diff against the prior Ph3 round's map, and (iii) the package-standard-delta set (see T3 below). The judgment layer processes (ii) + (iii); the grounding layer processes the full file.
- `skills/run-phase-3/SKILL.md` Phase 3 (Evaluator Engagement) documents the diff-scope rule and cross-references the scope separation to the v0.7.4 Rule 1 floor.

**Invariant preserved.** GROUNDING_PROTOCOL Rule 1 (full-file grounding reads at every rung) is untouched. The full file is read on every iteration; what narrows is the judgment layer, not the grounding layer.

**Invariant strengthened.** The judgment-scope boundary becomes computable (a hash diff) rather than judgmental ("did this change meaningfully?"). The Evaluator no longer silently re-judges stable prose at Ph3 cost.

**Evidence.** Structural — the scope mismatch between Rule 1 (full-file) and the current judgment layer (also full-file, but with much weaker motivation for it) is visible in the current `skills/run-phase-3/SKILL.md` where Step 3 reads "full-file, full envelope" without distinguishing the two layers.

**Priority.** Highest-leverage after P-9. P-10 is where the wall-clock per iteration actually collapses.

### P-11. Per-round check profile declared in the F6 dispatch plan

**Claim.** P-1's F6 dispatch plan already names *which agents* run and *at what model*; the dispatch plan is the natural surface to name *which checks* run and *at what depth*. Three canonical profiles span the Ph3 operating regime:

- **Ph3-refine** (default) — grounding (full-file) + Check 8 (full-file deterministic pre-filter plus judgment on diff) + sentence-level pass (diff) + contradiction check (new citations only) + P-stage spot-check.
- **Ph3-structural** — refine + narrative-structure pass (diff) + abstract-body spot-check. Invoked when the dispatch plan's `structural_delta_flag` is `true` (a section was reordered, a subsection was added, a thesis claim moved).
- **Ph3-deep** — refine + structural + graph-grounding overlay (full citation set) + IS-theory pass + Suchman register audit (where applicable). User-invoked at discretion, or Planner-suggested when the convergence vector stalls for three consecutive rounds under the refine profile.

**Change surface.**

- `references/ARTEFACT_FRONTMATTER_SCHEMA.md` F6 family gains `check_profile: Ph3-refine | Ph3-structural | Ph3-deep` and `structural_delta_flag: bool`. Field type: enum; absent-means-refine.
- `scripts/artefact_frontmatter_validate.py` validates the new fields.
- `agents/planner.md` Phase 0.6 gains **§0.6.3 Profile selection**: the Planner inspects the `paragraph_hash_map` diff against the prior round, sets `structural_delta_flag` when any section-level structural change is detected (section boundary moved, heading renamed, new subsection), defaults `check_profile` to `refine` (or `structural` if the flag is true), and surfaces the selection to the user at the F6 approval checkpoint.
- The user may override the profile at the approval checkpoint; Planner records the override in the dispatch-plan row's `notes`.
- `skills/run-phase-3/SKILL.md` §5 (Operational flow) documents the three profiles, their envelopes, and the user override.

**Invariant preserved.** User-gated checkpoint at dispatch-plan approval. No check is removed from Ph3's availability — `Ph3-deep` restores the v0.7.4.1 envelope exactly.

**Invariant strengthened.** Profile selection becomes visible, overridable, and audited — the F6 frontmatter carries the selection, so the Reflector's Phase 2f audit can flag a profile mismatch against the structural-delta flag (`R-Refl-PR-1 profile_delta_mismatch`, MINOR).

**Evidence.** Structural — P-1's F6 surface is the natural home for profile selection; the alternative (a separate trigger-enum slot) was explicitly rejected at the v0.7.4 gate (decision 6) in favour of round-scoped artefacts.

**Priority.** Ships with P-9. Without profile selection, P-9's disaggregation has no user-visible surface.

### P-12. Multi-signal convergence vector

**Claim.** The current `convergence_metric` is a single scalar with a two-round window, and it cannot distinguish two modes that matter: (i) lexical churn masking structural stability, where the scalar metric fails to close because surface edits keep landing but the argument is done moving; (ii) structural churn under lexical stability, where the scalar metric silently closes while the argument is still moving underneath. The redesign replaces the scalar with a four-component stability signature recorded per-iteration in `convergence_journal.jsonl`, and replaces the two-round window with a three-round window.

**Change surface.**

- `references/PHASE_PROTOCOL.md §3.3.2` (convergence contract) is rewritten: convergence fires when three consecutive iterations show every one of:
  - `grounding_clean: true`
  - `check8_aggregate ∉ {BLOCKER, MAJOR}`
  - `|findings_count_delta| ≤ 1`
  - `|line_delta| ≤ 0.5% of section length`
- `references/ARTEFACT_FRONTMATTER_SCHEMA.md` F1-family extends with the four-component signature block.
- `scripts/phase_state_validate.py` gains a convergence-vector validator.
- `scripts/migrate_convergence_journal_v075.py` (new) backfills prior `convergence_metric` scalars to the four-component shape where reconstructible (from existing F1/F2 frontmatter) and marks un-reconstructible rows as `[v0.7.5-BACKFILL-N/A]`.
- `skills/run-phase-3/SKILL.md` §6 (Convergence evaluation) documents the four-component logic.

**Invariant preserved.** The Ph3 → Ph3_converged transition remains user-gated via `TerminalSignoffRow`. Check 8 remains binding at signoff (§3.3.3). MCR admission semantics unchanged.

**Invariant strengthened.** Convergence becomes diagnostic: the Reflector's Phase 2g can attribute a stall to the specific component that is failing to stabilize, and the user sees which signal blocks closure.

**Evidence.** Structural — the scalar-metric failure modes (lexical churn, structural churn) are recurrent in narrative-heavy projects (INF3001H, INF3006Y) where surface edits and argument edits decouple. The v0.7.2 Check 8 surfaces already track the sub-components that a four-component vector would decompose cleanly.

**Priority.** Ships with P-9/P-10/P-11 as the tightening signal. Without P-12, the refine profile's convergence gate retains its known decoupling failure mode.

### P-13. Parallel subagent dispatch at Ph3

**Claim.** P-5 licenses subagent delegation under the I-SubAgent-1 invariant. The Ph3-refine envelope can be decomposed into three independently-computable probes — a grounding auditor (full-file), a Check 8 auditor (full-file deterministic pre-filter plus judgment on diff), and a contradiction-plus-sentence auditor (diff-only) — which the Evaluator can fan out in parallel and consolidate. Wall-clock per iteration collapses roughly threefold relative to sequential dispatch. The I-SubAgent-1 invariant ensures the Evaluator does not re-adjudicate subagent verdicts, so the consolidation step is a structured merge rather than a re-review.

**Change surface.**

- `agents/evaluator.md` Phase 3 (Evaluator Engagement) gains **§3.0 Parallel subagent dispatch** for the Ph3-refine profile: three `Task` dispatches in a single message with `subagent_type: "research-writing-harness-claude:evaluator"` and bounded scopes (grounding / Check 8 / contradiction+sentence). Each subagent returns a structured F1 findings block; the parent Evaluator merges them.
- `references/AGENT_CONTRACTS.md` Evaluator contract extends with a parallel-dispatch preconditions block (refine profile only; structural and deep profiles remain sequential because their check set does not decompose cleanly).
- I-SubAgent-1 is cited in the §3.0 prose as the soundness guarantee; the parent Evaluator documents that it does not re-adjudicate.
- `skills/run-phase-3/SKILL.md` §4 (Agent Dispatch) documents the parallel contract and the consolidation step.

**Invariant preserved.** Full-file grounding floor (each subagent reads its assigned file slice or the full file as required). The four-agent contract — the Evaluator role is played by the parent Evaluator, the subagents are bounded probes within that role, not independent adversarial agents. I-SubAgent-1 prohibits the parent from re-adjudicating.

**Invariant strengthened.** The Evaluator's role surface becomes decomposable at Ph3-refine, which is the regime where wall-clock-per-iteration matters most.

**Evidence.** Structural — the three probes are already orthogonal in the current envelope (they run on disjoint sub-surfaces of the file); serializing them is an implementation accident, not a soundness requirement.

**Priority.** Ships with P-9. Without P-13, the wall-clock gain from P-10 is partly re-spent on sequential subagent dispatch.

### P-14. Paragraph-hash verdict carryover cache

**Claim.** P-2's stability sub-mode generalizes beyond byte-stability: paragraphs whose SHA-256 is unchanged from the prior Ph3 round can inherit their prior verdict under a carryover cache keyed on `(paragraph_hash, check_id, model_used)`. Only paragraphs with changed hashes enter the judgment layer; only changed paragraphs pay the judgment cost. The cache invalidates on any of three conditions — a Claude-model change at the relevant phase-actor slot (capability shift), a package version bump (rule drift), or a Reflector-full finding that retroactively demotes a prior verdict. The `model_used` field added at v0.7.4 to the ledger log row is what makes this sound.

**Change surface.**

- `reviews/.session_cache/paragraph_verdict_cache.json` (new, gitignored) — hash-keyed cache of `{paragraph_hash, check_id, model_used} → verdict`. Written by the Evaluator at Ph3 iteration boundary; read by the Evaluator at Ph3 iteration entry.
- `scripts/paragraph_cache_validate.py` (new) verifies cache integrity against the three invalidation conditions; invalidates on any failure; logs invalidation to a `cache_invalidation.log` alongside the cache file.
- `agents/evaluator.md` Phase 3.2 (Judgment-layer execution) gains the **carryover rule**: for each unchanged paragraph, consult the cache; on hit with matching `model_used`, emit the cached verdict with an `[INHERITED]` tag in the finding's notes; on miss or mismatch, treat the paragraph as changed and re-judge.
- `agents/reflector.md` Phase 2f audit gains `R-Refl-CC-1 carryover_cache_staleness` (MINOR) for rows whose `model_used` diverges from the cached verdict's `model_used`.
- `.gitignore` extends with `reviews/.session_cache/paragraph_verdict_cache.json` and `reviews/.session_cache/cache_invalidation.log`.

**Invariant preserved.** Generator-as-sole-writer of manuscript (the cache is Evaluator-owned). Monotonicity of `phase_state.json` (the cache is not a ledger artefact). Full-file grounding floor (the cache is keyed on paragraph hash, not file hash, so grounding always re-reads).

**Invariant strengthened.** The §12.9 capability-inversion refusal gains a cache-level enforcement: carryover is refused across a model-family change.

**Evidence.** Structural — P-2's whole-manuscript stability sub-mode is the degenerate case of P-14's paragraph-level inheritance. The iter-7 observation that drove P-2 applies at finer granularity to every Ph3 round: paragraphs that did not change between iterations do not change verdict, and re-judging them is re-derivation of the prior round's output.

**Priority.** Ships with P-10/P-13. Without P-14, P-10's diff-scope still re-pays cost on every round's unchanged-but-uncached judgment layer.

### P-15. Demoted-check advisory surface

**Claim.** Checks demoted to Ph4 default still fire incidentally at Ph3 — a sentence-level pass may notice a Suchman register violation in a sentence it is rewriting; a contradiction check on new citations may notice an IS-theory alignment issue. These incidental firings are signal the package should capture without spending Ph3 budget on. The redesign routes them to an advisory surface: the finding is filed as an `F4-advisory` at severity MINOR, carries forward to Ph4 entry, and feeds the Reflector's recurrence audit. It does not block Ph3.

**Change surface.**

- `references/ARTEFACT_FRONTMATTER_SCHEMA.md` F4 family (currently `reflector_full_report`) extends with a `demoted_check_advisories` block — an array of `{check_id, finding_summary, severity, source_iteration}` rows. The block is append-only across Ph3 iterations.
- `agents/evaluator.md` Phase 3.2 gains the **incidental-finding rule**: when a Ph3-refine check produces a finding whose `check_id` matches a demoted check's identity (e.g., a sentence-level finding that identifies a Suchman register violation), the Evaluator files the finding into `F4-advisory` rather than into the primary F1 findings block.
- `agents/reflector.md` Phase 2g (recurrence audit) gains **§2g.3 Demoted-check recurrence**: scans the `F4-advisory` block across iterations, emits a cross-round recurrence finding when a demoted check fires in ≥ 2 iterations, and files the pattern under `lessons_learned.md` as a project-scoped lesson candidate.
- `skills/run-phase-4/SKILL.md` Ph4 entry consumes the `F4-advisory` block as first-class evidence: the demoted checks at Ph4 start from the Ph3-collected advisory as their prior, not from a cold start.

**Invariant preserved.** The four-agent contract. The Evaluator's role boundary (advisories are findings; the Evaluator files findings, it does not adjudicate). The Reflector's audit boundary (recurrence is what Phase 2g already does).

**Invariant strengthened.** Demotion stops being a blind spot. The package continues to collect signal on demoted classes at zero marginal Ph3 cost, and the Ph4 envelope starts pre-loaded with Ph3's incidental observations.

**Evidence.** Structural — the Reflector's Phase 2g already handles recurrence; extending it to cover the F4-advisory block is additive, not disruptive. The v0.7.4 economic package's P-3 frontmatter contract is the substrate the F4-advisory block writes into.

**Priority.** Ships with P-9. Without P-15, the demotion becomes a structural blind spot the Reflector cannot see.

### P-16. Ceiling-aware iteration budget

**Claim.** P-8's ceiling-lock ranking surfaces the termination option when a project's `default_final_phase == Ph3`. The redesign promotes P-8 from a menu-ordering hint to a **first-class iteration-budget metric** the Planner tracks and surfaces at every F6 approval. The Planner tracks the triple `{metric_distance_to_convergence, iterations_remaining, cost_budget_remaining}` and proactively offers a ceiling-lock at iteration *k* when the remaining metric distance is below thirty percent of the round-one distance. The user retains full override; the default is "offer, do not impose".

**Change surface.**

- `references/ARTEFACT_FRONTMATTER_SCHEMA.md` F6 family extends with the iteration-budget triple.
