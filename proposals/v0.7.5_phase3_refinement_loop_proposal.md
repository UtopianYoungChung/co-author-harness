# v0.7.5 Ph3 Refinement-Loop Proposal — Eight-Part Package (Revised v2)

## 0. Framing

This document files **eight proposed changes** to the `research-writing-harness-claude` plugin targeted at **v0.7.5 consideration**. The package is the direct successor of the v0.7.4 economic-efficiency package (proposals P-1 through P-8, filed 2026-04-21 and shipped in v0.7.4), and its proposals are numbered P-9 through P-16 to preserve a continuous proposal namespace across the two releases.

**Revision v2 disposition.** This is the post-review, post-advisor-consult revision of the proposal. The v1 file (archived at `proposals/archive/v0.7.5_phase3_refinement_loop_proposal_v1.md`) was subjected to an academic-peer-review-style evaluation, filed at `proposals/v0.7.5_phase3_refinement_loop_review_response.md`, producing 22 original Actions. Action A-GK-1 then routed the package through the plugin's `advisor-escalation` skill (consult filed at `proposals/v0.7.5_phase3_refinement_loop_advisor_consult.md`), producing three additional advisor-derived Actions — A-GK-3 (Ph4-deep classification falsification), A-GK-4 (RC freeze gate on A-GK-3), A-GK-5 (P-9a reframed as safety net). The revision carries all **25 Actions**. The scope decision (§6(a) of the response) was endorsed at the bucket level: ship-at-RC {P-9 + P-9a, P-10 with halo, P-11, P-13 with optimization-target declaration, P-15 with routing rule, register-audit soundness patch}; ship-provisionally {P-12, P-16} with thresholds marked `v0.7.5-provisional`; defer P-14 to v0.7.6. The threshold-derivation decision (§6(c)) was endorsed as the provisional path — thresholds marked `v0.7.5-provisional` with a v0.7.6 retuning milestone, rather than attempting a retrospective derivation on INF3001H / INF3006Y convergence-journal data under the current empirical basis.

The v0.7.4 package addressed a dispatch-and-artefact-economy problem: the same round was too expensive because its dispatch was unprincipled and its artefacts were prose-first. The v0.7.5 package addresses a distinct problem one layer up: **the Ph3 rung itself conflates two workloads whose amortization profiles are incompatible**. Refinement iteration (the many-shot inner loop) must amortize at the speed of local change; submission-readiness certification (the single-shot terminal audit) must amortize over the full manuscript. Packing both into Ph3 meant every local edit paid a global-audit cost it did not need, and Ph4 paid for checks that had already been spent.

The redesign is an **AORE-style role disaggregation** between Ph3 and Ph4. Ph3 keeps only the softgoals iteration can move — convergence and integrity. Ph4 absorbs the terminal-certification softgoals that neither improve nor decay with iteration. The checks still run; they run at the rung where they are dispositive. No invariant is demoted. The four-agent contract, the Grounding Protocol, the 15-field `SectionStateObject`, the 30-trigger enum, the MCR admission gate, the Check 8 TerminalSignoffRow binding, the `E-MA-CAPABILITY-INVERSION` refusal, and the I-SubAgent-1 invariant are all untouched. Every change is internal to the Ph3 envelope shape and the Ph3 ↔ Ph4 division of labour.

The package is shippable in two idempotent passes consistent with v0.7.4 discipline. Pass 1 is scaffold (schema extensions and dispatch-plan fields); Pass 2 is substantive (default-profile rewire, parallel subagent contract, Reflector recurrence-widening). P-14 (the paragraph-hash carryover cache) is deferred from v0.7.5 RC to v0.7.6 pending the per-check-class cache-key extension (Action A-CK-1); it is filed here at §3.P-14 as a v0.7.6-targeted appendix, not as a v0.7.5 deliverable. Dual-read through the v0.7.5 minor; removed at v0.7.6 RC.

## 1. Evidence base: the Ph3-as-submission-audit cost structure

The v0.7.4 package's evidence base was a single-round observation — the INF3006Y iter-7 stability pass — and that n=1 caveat carries visibly into the v0.7.4 CHANGELOG. The v0.7.5 package's evidence base is structural rather than empirical, and the distinction is deliberate but carries its own caveat: the problem this package addresses is **architectural** — it follows from the shape of the current Ph3 envelope, not from a cost measurement — so the falsification test lives at §7 (Instrumentation) rather than at §1. The honest framing (per Action A-IN-1) is **structural with empirical backfill deferred to §7's instrumentation** rather than structural-full-stop; the retrospective backfill from existing v0.7.4.1 convergence-journal and convergence-log data is executed as the v0.7.5 RC prep deliverable at `proposals/v0.7.5_instrumentation_baseline.md`.

The structural claim decomposes into four observations about the current (v0.7.4.1) Ph3 envelope:

**(a) Check-surface approximate-invariance under iteration.** The current Ph3 envelope runs the full SAFEGUARD layer (checks 1/4/5/7/8), the Coupling E.2 graph-grounding overlay on the full citation set, the seven-step Evaluator judgment pass on the full file, and (when invoked) the IS-theory, Suchman, narrative-structure, and abstract-body passes on every iteration. Many of these checks are **approximately iteration-invariant on most diffs**: the IS-theory pass typically returns the same five-element-model verdict when a single paragraph is tightened, the Suchman register audit typically returns the same seven-move inventory when a citation is re-paginated, and the graph-grounding overlay typically does not reshape when the bibliography is stable. The invariance is approximate, not strict — the IS-theory pass's verdict *will* move when the tightened paragraph anchors one of the five elements; the Suchman inventory *will* move when the re-paginated citation anchors one of the seven moves. The exception class is **anchor-dependence**: a local edit that, despite being paragraph-scoped, carries an anchor for a submission-readiness check becomes structural rather than local. P-11's profile surface (specifically the `structural_delta_flag`) is the mechanism for escalating from refine to structural when the dispatch layer detects an anchor-dependent change; a paragraph edit that re-anchors an IS-theory element or a Suchman move is, by the flag's definition, a structural change and escalates accordingly. The approximate-invariance claim is therefore a statement about the *default* behaviour under refine; it is not an assertion that the checks are invariant in all cases. These checks are *submission-readiness* checks: their purpose is to certify that the manuscript will survive external review, and they are typically invariant across the local edits that constitute a refinement iteration, with the exceptions handled by profile escalation.

**(b) Check-surface variance under iteration.** A smaller subset of checks does change verdict round-over-round. The grounding audit surfaces different findings as paragraphs are rewritten. The Check 8 accessibility overlay's Cadence-Flag, First-Use-Flag, and Signpost-Flag fire on local prose surface in ways that track local edits. The sentence-level pass (Bacon 9-point) and the contradiction check on newly introduced citation pairs move with local change. These are the checks whose *value* is highest inside the iteration loop because iteration is what moves their verdict.

**(c) Ph4-entry finding surprise.** Because the submission-readiness checks run at Ph3 today, they run under a dispatch that is not yet in the submission-readiness register. The Evaluator's Ph3 prompt frames each check as part of the convergence loop; its Ph4 prompt frames each check as terminal certification. The two framings produce different adversarial postures, and the Ph3-framed execution can surface incidentally-detected issues that are not dispositive at Ph3 but would have been dispositive at Ph4. The current envelope catches these issues *and* requires the Evaluator to file them as Ph3 findings, inflating the Ph3 findings register with signal that was never going to close at Ph3.

**(d) Iteration-rate ceiling.** The combined effect is an iteration rate ceiling. A many-iteration refinement loop wants wall-clock per iteration to be bounded by the cost of checks that move with the diff. The current envelope makes wall-clock per iteration bounded by the cost of the full submission audit. The user's directive — *"the speed and accuracy of each iteration matter"* — is incompatible with this ceiling, and the ceiling is not a cost measurement; it is a consequence of the check-surface packaging.

The v0.7.4 stability sub-mode (P-2) is the one existing counterweight. It collapses the envelope to grounding-plus-Check-8 when the manuscript is byte-stable. But P-2 is admitted only on byte-stability, which is the degenerate case. Between active refinement and byte-stability there is a large operating regime — small-delta rounds, where five percent of the file moved — for which no reduced envelope exists today. The v0.7.5 package fills this regime with a profile-conditioned default that defaults to refine, escalates to structural or deep only when the dispatch plan flags it, and inherits from P-2's hash-stability discipline rather than replacing it.

## 2. Theoretical reframe — the Ph3/Ph4 softgoal decomposition

Under a Strategic Rationale view, the softgoals Ph3 is currently asked to satisfice are three, not one:

- **Convergence** — the manuscript's prose surface stabilizes against a declared metric.
- **Integrity** — grounding, accessibility, and the non-negotiable register commitments hold at every iteration.
- **Submission-readiness** — the manuscript will survive external peer review on a breadth-first basis: IS-theory alignment, Suchman register discipline (where applicable), narrative arc, abstract-body coverage, bibliography contradiction integrity, graph-grounded citation alignment.

The first two are *iteration-shaped*: iteration moves the dial, and absence of iteration lets them decay. The third is *terminal-shaped*: it neither improves with iteration nor decays without it, and iteration is the wrong instrument for moving it. Packing all three into Ph3 is therefore not a cost problem but a role-assignment problem: the Ph3 adversarial agent has been asked to run two incompatible protocols simultaneously.

The redesign assigns the three softgoals to their rungs cleanly. Ph3 carries convergence and integrity; Ph4 absorbs submission-readiness. The Ph3 Evaluator operates in a refinement register — depth-first on local change, fast, high-iteration. The Ph4 Evaluator operates in a certification register — breadth-first on the full manuscript, slow, single-shot (or few-shot under the +50% MCR reserve). The separation is load-bearing in the same way the Generator-Evaluator separation is load-bearing: the same agent cannot carry two protocols without collapsing one into the other.

Two corollaries follow. First, the Ph3 iteration budget becomes economically rational: every iteration pays only for checks whose value is highest during iteration. Second, the Ph4 admission is no longer a formality gated on MCR — it is an active phase change where the adversarial register shifts from *is this stable?* to *is this defensible?* The Ph4 envelope remains the strict superset of Ph3 that v0.7.4.1 specifies; what changes is that the superset-minus-Ph3 surface now carries dispositive content rather than ratification of what Ph3 already spent budget on.

### 2.1 The register-switch soundness problem (and its patch)

The reviewer evaluation observed that the Generator-Evaluator separation, which is the package's foundational soundness move, works because **two agents occupy two files with two contracts and two invariants**. The Generator cannot adjudicate its own writing because the adjudication is happening in a different agent's address space, under a different prompt, under a different invariant surface. This is structurally stronger than asking **one Evaluator file to switch register between refinement and certification** depending on the active phase. The proposal at v1 relied on the weaker discipline without acknowledging it.

The revision adopts a two-part patch (Actions A-RG-1, A-RG-2). First, the F1 artefact frontmatter schema gains a new field, `adversarial_register: refinement | certification`, that the Evaluator declares at each dispatch. The Planner reads the field from the F6 dispatch plan; the Evaluator inherits it at Phase 0 and carries it forward into the F1 findings it produces. Second, the Reflector's Phase 2f contract audit gains a new sub-check, `R-Refl-RG-1 register_mismatch` (severity MAJOR), that at Ph3 iteration close verifies the declared register matches the filed findings. A Ph3-refine round that produces certification-register findings (e.g., IS-theory alignment verdicts on unmutated elements) trips the audit; a Ph4 round that produces refinement-register findings (e.g., sentence-level diffs without submission-readiness framing) also trips it.

The audit operates at the frontmatter level, not at prose level, so its marginal cost is bounded. The §7 instrumentation layer (see §7, metric **register-mismatch rate**) tests the empirical claim: the register-switch discipline must fire `R-Refl-RG-1` on fewer than five percent of Ph3-refine rounds across the first twenty v0.7.5 rounds. If the rate exceeds fifteen percent, the register-switch discipline is inadequate and P-9 must be reconsidered as a two-agent rather than a one-agent-two-registers design.

## 3. Proposals

### P-9. Ph3 / Ph4 check-surface disaggregation

**Claim.** The following checks should default to **Ph4** rather than Ph3: (a) full SAFEGUARD checks 1, 4, 5, 7 run depth-first; (b) IS-theory pass (Baird 9-step); (c) Suchman register audit; (d) public-interest accountability pass (when the project opts in); (e) Coupling E.2 graph-grounding overlay as a breadth sweep over the full citation set; (f) narrative-structure pass (Sexton 10-item arc); (g) check-abstract-body audit; (h) full-bibliography contradiction audit. Each remains **available at Ph3 as an explicit opt-in** through the F6 dispatch plan's `check_profile: Ph3-deep` declaration (see P-11). The checks that stay at Ph3 by default are the ones whose verdict is iteration-shaped: the grounding audit (non-negotiable full-file floor), the Check 8 accessibility overlay (binding at TerminalSignoffRow — the §13 commitment is load-bearing inside the iteration loop because accessibility drift is iteration-shaped), the sentence-level pass (Bacon 9-point) on changed paragraphs, the contradiction check on newly introduced citation pairs, and a P-stage register spot-check.

**Primary correctness guarantee.** Primary correctness of the `Ph3_converged` contract comes from the per-check falsification argument at §3.P-9.1 below (Action A-GK-5). The pre-MCR Ph3-deep pass at §3.P-9a provides a **safety net** for residual misclassifications the falsification argument missed — it is not the primary correctness guarantee. This division of labour is load-bearing: the falsification argument is filed at RC scope freeze as an auditable per-check soundness record; the pre-MCR pass catches only those checks whose classification survived the falsification but whose findings still, empirically, accrue during Ph3-refine iteration.

**Change surface.**

- `skills/run-phase-3/SKILL.md` Phase 3 (Evaluator Engagement) is rewritten: the canonical Ph3 envelope is the **refine** profile; the **structural** and **deep** profiles are invoked only on dispatch-plan declaration.
- `skills/run-phase-4/SKILL.md` Phase 3 absorbs the demoted checks as **mandatory** (not optional) Ph4 passes. The Ph4 envelope already contains most of these as part of its "strict superset" framing; the change is to mark them as Ph4-primary rather than Ph3-carried-forward.
- `references/PHASE_PROTOCOL.md §3.3` (Ph3 contract) is rewritten to enumerate the three profiles and to name the demoted checks explicitly as "Ph4-default, Ph3-opt-in" — with per-check classification provenance preserved from §3.P-9.1's falsification table.
- `references/PHASE_PROTOCOL.md §3.4` (MCR admission contract) is amended to enumerate the **pre-MCR-deep-pass requirement** alongside the existing `Ph3_converged` and non-stale requirements (Action A-MCR-2).
- `references/REVIEW_ORCHESTRATION.md` Steps 0.2 (Coupling E.2 overlay), 4 (SAFEGUARD layer), and 5 (theoretical-framework overlays) gain phase-conditioned gating: at Ph3 these steps run only when the dispatch profile is `structural` or `deep`; at Ph4 they run unconditionally.
- `agents/evaluator.md` gains a `§Adversarial register` section that names the two protocols (refinement / certification) and binds the agent's posture to the active phase; the register is declared in F1 frontmatter per Action A-RG-2.

**Invariant preserved.** Full-file grounding floor at every iteration. Check 8 at TerminalSignoffRow. Generator-as-sole-writer. User-gated checkpoints. The four-agent contract. The demoted checks still run — they run at Ph4, and Ph4 admission under MCR already requires every section to be `Ph3_converged`, so no section can reach submission without having paid for the demoted checks exactly once.

**Invariant strengthened.** The adversarial register at each rung becomes explicit rather than implicit. The Ph3 Evaluator's framing aligns with the softgoal the rung is actually moving. The classification of each deferred check is backed by a filed falsification argument (§3.P-9.1) rather than by an un-audited structural observation.

**Evidence.** Structural — §1(a,b,c,d) above. The v0.7.4.1 `skills/run-phase-3-stability/SKILL.md` already implements the principle for the degenerate byte-stable case; P-9 generalizes it to the full refinement regime. The classification correctness is certified per-check at §3.P-9.1.

**Priority.** Highest-leverage. P-9 is the architectural core of the package; P-10 through P-16 instrument and tighten around it.

### P-9.1 — Ph4-deep classification falsification (Action A-GK-3)

The advisor consult (`proposals/v0.7.5_phase3_refinement_loop_advisor_consult.md`) identified P-9 as carrying the highest residual convergence-guarantee risk in the revised scope, on the grounds that P-9 **redefines what `Ph3_converged` means** — the check-surface partition itself — whereas P-12 and P-16 gate *when* convergence is declared on a correctly-scoped surface. A wrong enumeration of Ph4-deep workloads causes convergence on the wrong check surface entirely; a wrong threshold causes too-early or too-late declaration on a correctly-scoped surface. The advisor's single recommended architectural move is a mandatory per-check falsification argument filed at RC freeze for each deferred workload. The revision adopts the recommendation in full. The classification-correctness claim, previously implicit in §1's structural observation, becomes an explicit, auditable per-check record here.

For each of the ten deferred workloads enumerated in P-9 — SAFEGUARD Check 1, SAFEGUARD Check 4, SAFEGUARD Check 5, SAFEGUARD Check 7, IS-theory pass (Baird), Suchman register audit, public-interest accountability pass, Coupling E.2 graph-grounding overlay, Sexton narrative-structure pass, check-abstract-body audit, and full-bibliography contradiction audit — the falsification argument records two sub-arguments and a disposition. (The eight-row framing in Action A-GK-3's original specification grouped SAFEGUARD 1/4/5/7 into a single logical row; §3.P-9.1 expands to a per-sub-check record so the falsification argument is per-check rather than per-family. The total row count is eleven.)

The two sub-arguments per row are:

1. **Edit class that could trigger a finding on this check during Ph3-refine iteration.** What class of paragraph-local or halo-scope edit, if it occurred during Ph3-refine, would produce a finding against this check?
2. **Why that edit class is unlikely during Ph3-refine, or why the Ph3-refine profile's existing checks would catch its symptoms.** If the edit class is structural (a new section, a re-paginated citation, a re-anchored argument), the sub-argument explains how P-11's `structural_delta_flag` would escalate the round from refine to structural *before* the deferred check's finding could accrue. If the edit class is paragraph-local, the sub-argument names the Ph3-refine check that would surface the finding under a different name.

The disposition column records the outcome of the falsification pass:

- **retained in Ph4-deep** — both sub-arguments hold; the check's findings are demonstrably independent of Ph3-refine iteration edits, or the Ph3-refine profile surfaces their symptoms under a different name. The check remains in the Ph4-deep bucket.
- **promoted to Ph3-refine in full** — sub-argument (2) fails; the edit class is plausible during Ph3-refine and no existing refine-side check surfaces the finding. The check is promoted to the Ph3-refine profile in its entirety, its full judgment layer running on every refine iteration.
- **promoted to Ph3-refine via lightweight proxy** — sub-argument (2) fails on judgment-layer grounds but a deterministic-counter pre-filter fires on the same signal class without running the full check. The proxy fires at Ph3-refine; the full check remains Ph4-default, now with the proxy feeding an F4-advisory (see P-15) on refine-surface firings.
- **under-powered → conservatively promoted to Ph3-refine with v0.7.6 retuning milestone** — the sub-arguments cannot be adjudicated under the current empirical basis (insufficient v0.7.4.1 convergence-journal evidence to assert either holds or fails). The check is conservatively promoted to Ph3-refine for v0.7.5 RC, and the classification is logged for retuning at v0.7.6 when convergence-journal data permits a proper empirical falsification pass.

The eleven-row falsification table is authored and filed in this §3.P-9.1 at v0.7.5 RC scope freeze. The table below was originally the **stub** the RC-freeze authoring pass fills in; under the **v0.8.0** single composed release, the disposition column was finalized at **`proposals/v0.8.0_upgrade_architecture.md` revision r19** (P2.8 / D-6) per the **v0.8.0 P2.8 scope-freeze** paragraph immediately under the table — the stub rows for sub-arguments (1)–(2) are retained verbatim; only the disposition column moved from *TBD at RC freeze* to binding text.

### P-9a. Pre-MCR Ph3-deep pass (safety net; Action A-MCR-1, reframed per A-GK-5)

Primary correctness comes from the per-check falsification argument at §3.P-9.1 (Action A-GK-3). ### P-12. Multi-signal convergence vector (thresholds v0.7.5-provisional)

### P-13. Parallel subagent dispatch at Ph3 (optimization target = wall-clock per iteration)

**Optimization target declaration (Action A-OT-1).** The optimization target is **explicitly wall-clock per iteration**, not aggregate compute. P-13 is honestly framed as a **cost-for-time trade**: three parallel subagents running full-file grounding reads consume 3× the token substrate per iteration, traded for ≈ 3× wall-clock collapse. The framing follows from the user directive driving the proposal — *"the speed and accuracy of each iteration matter"* — which is a wall-clock directive on its face; iteration is interactive and the user bears the wall-clock cost of each round. Aggregate compute is logged but not the optimization target.

**Budget-monitor line-item (Action A-OT-2).** The parallel-dispatch cost multiplier (aggregate compute per round / sequential-equivalent baseline) is logged per round in `convergence_journal.jsonl` under `parallel_dispatch_cost_multiplier: float`. If the multiplier exceeds 3.5× consistently, the trade is sharper than the wall-clock gain justifies and P-13 should be revisited — but this is an informed decision, not an accident.

**Opt-out flag (Action A-OT-3).** The F6 dispatch plan gains `parallel_dispatch: bool` (default `true` under the Ph3-refine profile). Projects for which aggregate compute is the binding constraint (e.g., a long-running v0.7.6 refinement cycle under a capped budget) can set `parallel_dispatch: false` and fall through to sequential dispatch, trading wall-clock for aggregate compute.

### P-14. Paragraph-hash verdict carryover cache (DEFERRED to v0.7.6)

**Disposition.** P-14 is **deferred** from v0.7.5 RC to v0.7.6 per Action A-CK-2, pending the per-check-class cache-key extension (Action A-CK-1). The deferral is explicit in the revised shipping scope at §4. P-14's soundness patch is non-trivial, its implementation complexity is the highest in the package, and its absence does not block the wall-clock gain from P-10 or the convergence tightening from P-12. P-14 rejoins the package at v0.7.6 when the check-class-aware key is designed and implemented.

**Claim (deferred).** P-2's stability sub-mode generalizes beyond byte-stability: paragraphs whose SHA-256 is unchanged from the prior Ph3 round *can* inherit their prior verdict under a carryover cache, but only for paragraph-local checks. Cross-paragraph checks (contradiction, signposting) require a round-scoped context that naive paragraph-hash keying fails to capture — a paragraph whose hash is unchanged can legitimately change verdict when a distant paragraph introduces a contradiction with it, or when a new signposting move elsewhere shifts its role in the argument.

**Per-check-class cache key (Action A-CK-1, v0.7.6-targeted).** The revised cache key is check-class-aware:

- **Paragraph-local checks** (sentence-level, first-use, paragraph-local Check 8 sub-checks) use `(paragraph_hash, check_id, model_used)`. Cache hits are sound under paragraph hash stability alone.
- **Cross-paragraph checks** (contradiction, Check 8 Signpost-Flag and Worked-Example-Flag, narrative-structure) use `(paragraph_hash, check_id, model_used, diff_scope_fingerprint)` where `diff_scope_fingerprint` is the SHA-256 of the sorted changed-paragraph-id set. Cache hits are sound only when the current round's cross-paragraph context matches the cached context.

The refinement softens the reviewer's exact-match round-fingerprint (which would rarely hit across iterations) to a **diff-scope subset key**: cross-paragraph checks cache-hit when the current round's diff-set is a subset of the prior round's diff-set for cross-paragraph checks, and match by `(paragraph_hash, check_id, model_used)` alone for paragraph-local checks. The check-class tag comes from the `halo_scope` field added in Action A-HL-2.

**Change surface (v0.7.6).** Same as v1's §3.P-14 specification, amended with the per-check-class key. Filed here at v0.7.5 as an **appendix** to document the deferred design; the v0.7.6 release will land the implementation.

**Invariant preserved.** Generator-as-sole-writer of manuscript (the cache is Evaluator-owned). Monotonicity of `phase_state.json` (the cache is not a ledger artefact). Full-file grounding floor (the cache is keyed on paragraph hash, not file hash, so grounding always re-reads).

**Invariant strengthened (at v0.7.6).** The §12.9 capability-inversion refusal gains a cache-level enforcement: carryover is refused across a model-family change.

**Evidence.** Structural — P-2's whole-manuscript stability sub-mode is the degenerate case of P-14's paragraph-level inheritance. The cross-paragraph-contamination concern (reviewer §3, P-14) is what pushes P-14 from v0.7.5 to v0.7.6: the per-check-class key is the soundness patch, and its design is non-trivial.

**Priority.** Deferred to v0.7.6.

### P-15. Demoted-check advisory surface (with routing rule)

**Claim.** Checks demoted to Ph4 default still fire incidentally at Ph3 — a sentence-level pass may notice a Suchman register violation in a sentence it is rewriting; a contradiction check on new citations may notice an IS-theory alignment issue. These incidental firings are signal the package should capture without spending Ph3 budget on. The redesign routes them to an advisory surface: the finding is filed as an `F4-advisory` at severity MINOR, carries forward to Ph4 entry, and feeds the Reflector's recurrence audit. It does not block Ph3.

**Routing determinism rule (Action A-RT-1).** A finding's **primary evidence** is the check class under which the Evaluator first detected the issue (typically the check that fired its pre-filter). A finding whose primary evidence is `sentence-level-pass` is filed to F1 even if it also exhibits a demoted-class pattern. A finding whose primary evidence is a demoted-class pattern that the Evaluator noticed incidentally while running a different check is filed to F4-advisory. The Evaluator declares the routing decision at filing time with a `routing_rationale: "primary_evidence=<check_id>"` field in the finding frontmatter. A worked routing example is added to `agents/evaluator.md §3.2`.

**Routing audit (Action A-RT-2).** The Reflector's Phase 2f audit gains `R-Refl-RT-1 routing_ambiguity` (severity MINOR): a finding whose `routing_rationale` field is missing, or whose primary-evidence `check_id` does not match the filing family (F1 for primary, F4-advisory for incidental), trips the audit.

**Check 8 variance probe (Action A-AC-1).** Under refine, Check 8 runs its A–F sub-checks via the accessibility-overlay skill; the D (Signpost-Flag) and E (Jargon-Density-Flag) sub-checks are the two that have structural character and overlap with the demoted Sexton and abstract-body audits. The advisory is filed to `F4-advisory` under the P-15 surface.

**Change surface.**
