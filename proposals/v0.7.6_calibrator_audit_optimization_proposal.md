# v0.7.6 Optimization Proposal — Plugin-Calibrator Three-Axis Audit Response

## 0. Framing

This document files a **tiered optimization package** for the `research-writing-harness-claude` plugin, grounded in a deterministic three-axis audit performed on 2026-04-22 against the shipped v0.7.4.1 substrate. The audit consisted of three calibrator passes — `audit-package-quality`, `audit-package-economics`, `audit-package-speed` — reading a single shared probe output to keep the findings mutually consistent. The deterministic verdict across all three axes was `pass` with four MINORs, zero MAJOR, zero BLOCKER. The plugin is structurally sound.

Optimization is therefore **surgical, not structural**. Every recommendation in this package preserves the four-agent contract, the Lifecycle-Phase Ladder rung semantics, the 15-field `SectionStateObject`, the 30-trigger enum, the Grounding Protocol, and the Manuscript Convergence Report admission gate. The proposals compose into five tiers (A–E) ordered by impact × inverse-risk, and the sequencing recommendation at §6 stages them across three successive releases (v0.7.5 / v0.7.5-RC / v0.7.6) so that each tier's effects can be measured before the next is authored.

The thesis of the audit reading is this: three of the four MINOR findings are downstream of a single assumption surface (the plugin ships without `.plugin-efficiency.json`, so the probe runs against documented defaults), and the fourth is downstream of a scheduled back-compat state (the `run-tier-*` aliases remain live through the v0.7.4 minor). Pinning the configuration file and honouring the scheduled alias removal clears three of four MINORs without authoring a single contract edit. The remaining structural edits — **Tier C** — target the two largest token-concentration surfaces (the Evaluator prompt body; the three overlapping Ph3/Ph4 runners) and are the highest-leverage authorship moves in the package. **Tier D** addresses the single legitimate code hot-spot. **Tier E** names two candidates I recommend *watching* rather than acting on.

The single most important operational fact this audit surfaces is **not** the single-round $1.89 cost figure. It is the **`subagent_dispatch_multiplier: 10.0`** annotation on the economics axis: a mis-dispatched Ph3 iteration re-ingests the prompt surface across ten subagent calls, turning a $1.89 round into a $19 round. The v0.7.4 economic-efficiency package (P-1 through P-8) correctly targets this risk surface; this proposal complements rather than replaces it.

## 2. Tier A — pin the assumptions (target v0.7.5)

**Claim.** The plugin ships without `.plugin-efficiency.json` at the harness root, so every efficiency probe runs against documented defaults: executor at `claude-sonnet-4-5` and $3/Mtok input, orchestrator at `claude-opus-4-7` and $15/Mtok input, throughput 500 tok/s. These defaults are historical conveniences, not rate-card truths. The calibrator flags this as `efficiency_config_missing` (MINOR) and annotates `assumed_rates_verified: false` on every report. Three of the four MINOR findings in the current audit are downstream of this single gap.

**Change surface.**

- Ship `.plugin-efficiency.json` at the plugin root. The config should echo the §12.9 `MODEL_ALLOCATION.md §2` slot assignments: Sonnet 4.6 at executor slots, Haiku 4.5 at Reflector-lightweight (during its 30-day pilot), Opus 4.7 at the four capability-inversion floor slots (Evaluator-Ph2, Evaluator-Ph3, Evaluator-Ph4, Reflector-full-Ph4). Set `input_tokens_per_second_assumed` to a measured throughput rather than the 500 tok/s default — a single calibrated measurement against current-generation Opus 4.7 input processing will do.
- The config should not duplicate `MODEL_ALLOCATION.md` as the authoritative slot table; it should reference it. The efficiency config only needs to pin the per-Mtok rates so the probe's arithmetic is anchored to reality.

**Invariant preserved.** All contracts. This is a pure configuration surface.

**Invariant strengthened.** The MINOR `efficiency_config_missing` clears. The $1.89 / round figure becomes quotable externally. The 4.38-minute wall-clock shifts to its believable range (~1 minute at real Opus 4.7 throughput). The Haiku-at-Reflector-lightweight placement pulls the cost contribution of the ~11 lightweight-Reflector-eligible skills downward once the pilot lifts the placement from provisional to confirmed.

**Evidence.** Audit report `writing_harness_economics_audit_v0.7.4.1.md` §5 ("The assumed-rates caveat"). `writing_harness_speed_audit_v0.7.4.1.md` §5 ("The 4.38-minute wall-clock is throughput-sensitive"). Both reports quote `assumed_rates_verified: false` and `config_source: defaults`.

**Priority.** Highest impact-per-hour in the package. Half a day including verification.

**Sensitivity.** None. Pinning a config cannot destabilise a contract surface.

## 4. Tier C — two structural edits that preserve every contract (target v0.7.6)

These are the highest-leverage authorship moves in the package. Both target the two largest token-concentration surfaces identified in the economics-axis audit. Both preserve every invariant in `PHASE_PROTOCOL.md` and every user-gated checkpoint.

### P-C1. Demote `REVIEW_ORCHESTRATION` step exposition out of the Evaluator prompt body

**Claim.** The Evaluator prompt at 7.6k tokens carries an expanded exposition of `REVIEW_ORCHESTRATION.md` Steps 0a–8.5 inline. The canonical spec lives at `references/REVIEW_ORCHESTRATION.md`. The exposition in the prompt body is a re-statement, not an independent source. Replacing the expansion with a pointer to the reference file preserves full reachability while collapsing the prompt surface.

**Change surface.**

- Edit `agents/evaluator.md`: replace the expanded Steps 0a–8.5 block with a condensed invocation-protocol block — "Execute `REVIEW_ORCHESTRATION.md` Steps 0a–8.5; stop and surface findings at each step's declared user-gate" — plus a one-line precondition checklist per step rather than the full per-step exposition.
- The Evaluator's invariant contract — sole reviewer, never writes prose, produces Check 8 aggregate at Ph3 TerminalSignoffRow — is re-stated at the prompt's head and preserved unchanged.
- The deterministic pre-filter references (`DETERMINISTIC_CHECKS §9b` for Check 8 counters) are preserved inline because they name artefacts the Evaluator must read before it can adjudicate.

**Token saving.** ≈ 3–4k tokens (roughly 40–50 % of the Evaluator prompt body). At orchestrator-tier cost of $15/Mtok, the saving translates to roughly $0.05–0.06 per Evaluator invocation at current rates.

**Invariant preserved.** Every Step 0a–8.5 remains reachable by reference. The Evaluator's contract (never writes prose; Opus-4.7 capability-inversion floor at Ph2/Ph3/Ph4) is unchanged.

**Evidence.** `writing_harness_economics_audit_v0.7.4.1.md` §3 ("The Evaluator (7.6k) and Generator (6.8k) are the cheaper pair and the safer targets"). Prompt-token breakdown from the shared probe per-artefact table.

**Priority.** 2–3 hours authorship; second-highest leverage in the package after Tier A.

**Sensitivity.** MEDIUM. The Evaluator prompt is the adversarial-review floor; any edit must be reviewed by someone who can verify that the step-by-step contract remains fully reachable through the reference path. Recommend paired review before landing.

### P-C2. Include-by-reference collapse across `run-phase-3`, `run-phase-3-stability`, `run-phase-4`

**Claim.** The three SKILLs carry ~5k tokens each (5,077 + 4,973 + 5,007 = 15,057 tokens combined) with substantial envelope overlap — the stability sub-mode is a reduced envelope of the full Ph3 pass, and Ph4 is a strict superset of Ph3. Much of the shared envelope language is re-stated across all three SKILLs rather than factored into a common reference. A shared reference fragment would preserve each SKILL's distinct invocation contract while collapsing the duplicated envelope surface.

**Change surface.**

- Author a new reference fragment at `references/PHASE3_PHASE4_COMMON_ENVELOPE.md` containing the shared envelope language: seven-step judgment pass structure, SAFEGUARD layer invocation, Check 8 aggregation rule, the `convergence_metric` declaration / reading contract, the Coupling E.2 graph-grounding overlay dispatch, and the Reflector-Phase-2f / Phase-2g audit hooks.
- Edit each of the three SKILLs to replace its copy of the shared envelope with a one-line pointer to the new reference fragment, retaining only the SKILL-specific differentiators:
  - `run-phase-3`: the unbounded iterate-until-stable loop, the `[CONVERGENCE-STABLE]` two-consecutive-round rule, the TerminalSignoffRow write contract.
  - `run-phase-3-stability`: the byte-stability precondition (SHA-256 hash match), the reduced Evaluator envelope (grounding audit + Check 8 deterministic counters only), the trigger-30 escalation path to full Ph3.
  - `run-phase-4`: the external-verifier REQUIRED escalation, the G.4 sign-off gate, the Reflector-full close-out dispatch, the Coupling D M5 wiki-ingest hook, EG-1 and EG-7 as the only legal demotion paths.

**Token saving.** ≈ 5–7k tokens on any Ph3 or Ph4 invocation. Concentrated on the orchestrator tier; at $15/Mtok that translates to ~$0.08–0.11 per invocation in direct cost. Because Ph3 is the unbounded iterate-until-stable phase, a project doing N Ph3 iterations saves N × this figure across a round.

**Invariant preserved.** Each SKILL retains its own invocation contract and its own distinct invariants. The shared fragment is *reference-only* — it is not re-executed, it is read as authoritative specification. `PHASE_PROTOCOL.md §3.3` remains the canonical rung-semantics source.

**Evidence.** `writing_harness_economics_audit_v0.7.4.1.md` §3 (top-ten token contributors; ranks 5, 6, 7 are the three runners). `writing_harness_economics_audit_v0.7.4.1.md` §6 ("Pruning leverage is in two places").

**Priority.** 4–6 hours authorship (1–2 hours for the reference fragment, 1–2 hours per SKILL edit, 1 hour verification). Highest-leverage on the Ph3 loop specifically, where per-iteration ingest is compounded.

**Sensitivity.** MEDIUM-HIGH. The three runners' distinct invariants must be preserved cleanly against the shared fragment. Recommend that the reference fragment be authored first, reviewed in isolation, and then the three SKILL edits be performed in a single session with paired review.

### Tier C combined impact

Combined token saving: ≈ 8–11k tokens, concentrated on the orchestrator tier. Per-round cost impact: ≈ 10–12 % reduction on a full four-phase climb, materially higher on any Ph3-heavy iteration. Authorship cost: 6–9 hours total including review.

## 5. Tier D — the one real code refactor (target v0.7.6)

**Claim.** `scripts/sk20_overlay_run.py` at `CC = 48` is the only maintainability outlier in the Python corpus that is *not* one-shot migration code. The four other top-complexity modules (`coupling_readiness_check.py`, `migrate_v073_to_v074_tier_to_phase.py`, `migrate_v055_to_v060.py`, `artefact_frontmatter_validate.py`) are either migration scripts that age out at v0.7.5 RC or one-coupling validators whose branching load reflects schema translation rather than runtime logic. `sk20_overlay_run.py` sits on the hot path of Ph3 overlay dispatch — the branching load is runtime, not translational, and the MI-proxy of 0.0 reflects a genuine concentration of control flow in one function.

**Change surface.**

- Refactor `scripts/sk20_overlay_run.py` into 3–4 cohesive functions along the natural dispatch boundaries. Target per-function CC ≤ 15 and module MI-proxy ≥ 30.
- Preserve the overlay's I/O contract (inputs: manuscript hash, prior-round check8 aggregate, accessibility-overlay subcheck vector; outputs: overlay verdict and finding list). The public behaviour is unchanged; only the internal decomposition moves.
- Add or extend a unit-test module for the overlay dispatcher if none currently exists; the decomposition is a natural test boundary.

**Invariant preserved.** Overlay I/O contract. The calibrator's `quality/max_cyclomatic_complexity` MINOR should clear if the ceiling holds at 15 or the refactor is accompanied by a documented threshold relaxation in `.plugin-efficiency.json`.

**Invariant strengthened.** The MI-proxy at 0.0 lifts off the floor; future contract changes that touch the overlay become safer to author.

**Evidence.** `writing_harness_quality_audit_v0.7.4.1.md` §2 ("Python hot-spots — concentrated, not pervasive"). Per-module table from the shared probe, ranks 1 and 10.

**Priority.** 3–4 hours for the refactor including test authorship. Maintainability investment, not performance: the savings register on the *next* contract change rather than on the current one.

**Sensitivity.** LOW-MEDIUM. Unit-test coverage before the refactor is the main precondition. If no tests currently exist for the dispatcher, authoring them is part of the refactor scope.

## 6. Tier E — defer, but watch

Two candidates I recommend not acting on in the v0.7.6 window.

**P-E1. Shrinking the Reflector or Planner prompts (DEFER).** The 18.2k Reflector and 16.9k Planner prompts encode structurally non-negotiable contract surface: the confirmation-failed historical audit, the Phase 2d/2e/2f/2g audit blocks, the three-filter plugin-update gatekeeper, the 15-field `SectionStateObject` contract, the 30-trigger enum, the 7-field log-row shape (with absent-means-null `model_used`), the MCR admission gate, and the I-SubAgent-1 invariant. Compressing these bodies would be a contract change, not a cosmetic edit. Hold until a future contract simplification — trigger-enum consolidation, for instance, or a MODEL_ALLOCATION table that can be referenced rather than re-expressed — opens headroom.

**P-E2. Orchestrator-level `batch-dispatchable` contract tag to lift parallelisability (DEFER).** The architecture already enforces the right split: the seven parallelisable executors under the current heuristic are exactly the Evaluator's diagnostic-probe cluster, and the sixteen sequenced executors are the craft-pass chains (`sentence-level-pass` → `narrative-structure-pass` → `p-stage-checker`; three-pass wiki-grounding chain). Lifting the fraction would require a Planner-level `batch-dispatchable` tag and a fan-out mechanism. Worthwhile only if wall-clock budgets start mattering more than they do at current throughput. File as a **P-9 candidate** for a future release (v0.7.7 or later) if the need materialises.

Watch signal: a future round where the reported `parallelisable_fraction` drops *below* 0.304 without a clear architectural justification is the trigger to revisit P-E2 earlier.

## 8. Non-goals and explicit deferrals

This package does **not** propose:

- Compression of the Reflector or Planner prompts (P-E1 — deferred; contract-surface-bearing).
- Introduction of a `batch-dispatchable` contract tag (P-E2 — deferred; architectural).
- Modification of the Grounding Protocol, the four-agent contract, the Lifecycle-Phase Ladder rung semantics, the `SectionStateObject` schema, or the trigger enum.
- Any change to `PHASE_PROTOCOL.md §3.3.3` (the Check 8 accessibility gate at Ph3 TerminalSignoffRow).
- Any change to the MCR admission contract at §12.4.
- Re-litigation of the v0.7.4 economic-efficiency package P-1 through P-8. Those proposals address the `×10` dispatch-multiplier risk surface through operational mechanisms (dispatch-plan authorship, state caching, iteration batching); this proposal addresses the prompt-surface token base through structural authorship edits. The two packages are complementary and do not overlap.

## 9. Honesty notes carrying forward from the audit

- `assumed_rates_verified: false` and `config_source: defaults` on every probe reading in this audit. The $1.89 / round figure and the 4.38-minute wall-clock are unpinned. Tier A clears this.
- The calibrator's `prompt_clarity_score: 0.229` is a vocabulary mismatch between the writing-harness heading lexicon (`## What you do`, `## What you output`, `## When to invoke this skill`) and the calibrator's seven-substring recogniser (`procedure`, `output contract`, `when to use`, `activation`, `example`, `usage`, `inputs`). This is not treated as a defect in this proposal because the writing-harness headings are internally consistent and legible on their own terms. A decision to reconcile the lexicon (either by renaming the SKILL headings to match the calibrator, or by widening the calibrator's tag list) is a cross-plugin coordination matter and is filed separately if pursued.
- The memoised-DFS longest-path algorithm at `plugin_calibrator/efficiency.py:439` is an approximation, not exhaustive enumeration (longest-simple-path on a cyclic digraph is NP-hard). The `max_subagent_chain_depth: 19` value should be read as *"there exists at least one 19-step reachability chain"*, not as *"every dispatch traverses 19 hops"*. Tier B's regression gate should use the same algorithm so the ceiling is measured consistently with the probe.
