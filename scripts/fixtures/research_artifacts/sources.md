# Synthetic research evidence packet

These materials are invented test inputs. They are not published research and
must never be cited as empirical evidence outside this benchmark.

## S1: Representation proposal, paragraph 1
A representation can expose which actor depends on another actor for a decision.
Exposing that dependency does not transfer authority or change the work itself.
The proposal is to compare two representations while holding the work procedure,
participants and available information constant. It remains an open question
whether either representation helps participants notice unresolved concerns.

## S2: Pilot notes, paragraph 1
In a synthetic pilot, four participants inspected each representation. Three
participants using representation A identified the unresolved approval concern;
two using representation B identified it. Participants were assigned by arrival
order. No timing data or pre-task ability measures were recorded. This small,
non-random assignment does not establish a causal benefit or general superiority.

## S3: Contrasting observation, paragraph 1
In a second synthetic session, participants said representation B made the
responsible decision maker easier to identify. The session did not record concern
detection counts and used a different task. Its observation cannot be pooled
with the first pilot as another count of the same outcome.

## S4: Missing evidence, paragraph 1
No supplied source defines the proposed construct "adaptive accountability".
The construct may be investigated at the proposal stage; no settled definition,
validated scale or reported treatment effect is available in this packet.
