## The five measures

Apply section by section, before wording discussion. A section that fails one measure is not ready for craft polish on the others.

### 1. Vocabulary audit

Every technical term must trace to a source byte or the professor's transcript. Anything else is "reduction"-class: earn it in view of the reader, or drop it.

Load-bearing terms in the calibration abstract are source-owned or professor-owned (`warrant` = transcript sense; the three grounds = PDF bytes). Project-coined names are not banned; they are earned only after the act they name is visible (measure 3).

**Fail:** invented vocabulary that survived from scaffolding (`earning`, `reduction`, `start-and-stop`, calling the contribution a `problem` when no source does and the paper offers no solution).

### 2. Novelty shape

Each contribution site names the prior practice before claiming the strain: **X has always been done; this paper puts X under strain where Y.**

Do not imply the field lacked the practice. Do not teaser. Honest novelty states what existed and where it is tested.

### 3. Ordering

Reader sequence: **familiar situation → felt gap → concrete case → contribution name.**

Check by scanning for project vocabulary appearing before its referent. Name a coined contribution once, only after the act or relation it names is visible (EIBU by sequence, not by gloss).

### 4. Gap-before-claim

Each exclusion, finding, or contribution claim is preceded by the gap it closes. For the Paper 2 body: §3's exclusions and §4's findings each follow the gap they close. Do not lead with the cut.

### 5. One job per sentence

One grammatical job, one argumentative job. The Ver03 choppiness report's A1–A5 are this rule's absence and are reusable as the section-level defect census: announcement–count mismatch, forward refs, definitional echo, meta-voice, broken grammatical bridges.

Active analyst/paper agency is the default voice for this measure. C-7 still protects idiolect: one job is not a warrant to flatten sentence-length signature.

## Evaluator / Generator contract

- **Generator.** Apply the five before C-1…C-8 craft. Do not introduce a technical term that fails measure 1. Do not name a contribution before its act (measure 3). Do not stack two jobs in one sentence to "save" a round (measure 5).
- **Evaluator.** Run the five as the section-level check set before wording discussion. Argument coherence (`ARGUMENT_COHERENCE.md`, SAFEGUARD Check 9) is a separate mandatory obligation, not a sixth umbrella: it asks whether each sentence advances its paragraph and whether the document still carries its commitments. A U1–U5 finding and an AC-n finding can sit on the same sentence. Cite the umbrella number in the finding (`[MAJOR — U1 vocabulary]`, `[MAJOR — U4 gap-before-claim]`). Locator and remediation still required.
- **Planner.** Record that the five apply. Do not treat a C-n suspension as a suspension of the matching umbrella unless Joseph names the umbrella.
- **Reflector.** Recurrence of a U1–U5 failure across rounds is a lesson candidate. Do not re-flag a closed vocabulary item as a new C-6 finding without naming the umbrella.

Finding codes U1…U5 do not replace C-n codes. A sentence can carry both (`[MAJOR — U1 / C-6]`).
